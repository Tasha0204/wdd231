Non-Profit Membership

Benefits of the Non-Profit Level:

  • Digital Directory: Basic inclusion in the Chamber's official business directory.
  • Training & Education: Free access to educational workshops and professional webinars.
  • Strategic Alliances: Networking spaces to connect with private companies and seek sponsorships.
  • Admission Fee: Waived (Subject to legal status verification).

Bronze Membership

Benefits of the Bronze Level:

  • Business Profile: Standard directory listing with direct links to your website and social media.
  • Trust Badge: Digital "Active Member" seal to display on your business website.
  • Networking Access: Admission to our general monthly business networking events.
  • Initial Support: Special discounts on business management and digital marketing courses.

Silver Membership

Benefits of the Silver Level:

  • Includes all Bronze Level benefits.
  • Monthly Advertising: Featured advertising slot in the Chambers monthly email newsletter.
  • VIP Passes: 2 complimentary tickets to major annual conferences and gala dinners.
  • Internal Promotion: Permission to publish exclusive offers and discounts for other network members.
  • Venue Discounts: 20% discount on renting the Chambers meeting rooms and event spaces.

Gold Membership (Premium)

Benefits of the Gold Level:

  • Includes all previous membership benefits.
  • Maximum Visibility: Prominent logo display on the Home Page (Spotlight section) and top priority in directory searches.
  • Exclusive Sponsorship: First priority to become a main sponsor at major trade shows and high-profile events.
  • Commercial Leadership: Full access to sector committees with voting rights on local commercial initiatives.
  • Insider Insights: Free access to exclusive economic reports, market trends, and statistical studies.
